## 6. Internal admission versus scientific truth

A correct admission gate determines
whether a declared contract is met.

It does not, by logical necessity,
prove that arbitrary stronger claims
about the admitted candidate are true.

For example, the supported label:

REGIMEN INTERNO ADMITIDO BAJO
LAB-FEN-001-v1 Y DMO-004

cannot be silently rewritten as:

externally validated physical regime.

The latter requires independent
evidence outside the frozen contract.

This is a scope distinction,
not a newly observed laboratory failure.

## 8. Formal proof obligation

For a specific claim C and
supporting evidence E under assumptions A,
a rigorous formal promotion requires
a valid relation of the form:

A and E entail C.

For empirical claims, the relevant
support relation also depends on
uncertainty, study design, and
the stated inference framework.

Merely storing links from E to C
does not prove that relation.

A future formal checker would need
defined claim semantics and
machine-checkable scope restrictions.

No such general checker is
demonstrated in the reviewed sources.
