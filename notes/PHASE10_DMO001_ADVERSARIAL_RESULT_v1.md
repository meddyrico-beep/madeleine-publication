## 1. Audited construction

DMO-001 declares a finite family of reading marks:

E = {e_1,e_2,e_3}

and a distinguished internal reference:

sub_0.

It defines relative readings through:

L(e_i) = d(sub_0,e_i).

In the concrete instance:

L(A)=1,
L(B)=2,
L(C)=3.

It then forms:

Delta L(A,C)
=
L(C)-L(A)
=
2.

The demo explicitly denies any required interpretation in:

metres,
seconds,
energy,
or another external physical magnitude.

---

## 2. Internal reference role

The construction is logically consistent as a designation of a common reference.

Once sub_0 and L are declared, every reading in the example is organized relative to the same reference.

Therefore the statement:

sub_0 can function as a common internal reference

SURVIVES.

This is an existence-by-construction result.

It is not yet a necessity or uniqueness result.

---

## 3. Relabeling audit

DMO-001 replaces:

A,B,C

by:

X,Y,Z

while preserving:

L(X)=1,
L(Y)=2,
L(Z)=3.

Then:

Delta L(X,Z)
=
3-1
=
2.

This is correct.

SURVIVES.

However, the invariance follows directly from preserving the values of L under the relabeling.

Therefore this establishes invariance under a value-preserving renaming.

It does not establish invariance under arbitrary transformation of the reading structure.

---

## 4. Central adversarial distinction

There are two distinct claims:

A.

sub_0 is the reference designated by the MADELEINE construction.

B.

sub_0 is mathematically forced, unique or canonical independently of that designation.

DMO-001 establishes A.

It does not establish B.

The construction begins by choosing sub_0 as the reference.

No theorem inside DMO-001 proves that another reference could not be selected.

Therefore:

designated internal reference:

SURVIVES.

Mathematically unique or necessary origin:

NOT ESTABLISHED.

---

## 5. Meaning of "canonical"

Within MADELEINE, the word:

canonical

can safely mean:

the reference fixed by the declared internal convention.

DMO-001 does not establish the stronger mathematical meaning:

unique,
choice-free,
invariantly distinguished by the underlying structure,
or forced by a universal property.

A future version should make this distinction explicit if the word "canonical" could be read in the stronger sense.

---

## 6. External mathematical positioning

The use of a selected reference point or origin to express other points relatively is standard mathematics.

In affine geometry, fixing a point allows points to be represented by displacement vectors relative to that point.

Likewise, an affine coordinate frame contains a selected initial point together with a basis.

Therefore the isolated reference construction of DMO-001 is not treated as a new mathematical invention.

The possible MADELEINE-specific issue is the role assigned to sub_0 inside the wider architecture.

---

## 7. Codomain weakness

DMO-001 writes:

L(e_i)=d(sub_0,e_i)

and describes d as an internal reading relation.

The concrete example assigns numerical values:

1,2,3.

Therefore:

L(C)-L(A)

is well-defined in that instance.

However, the general construction does not explicitly specify the codomain of L or algebraic properties of d.

To define subtraction generally, a future formalization should specify a structure supporting the required difference operation.

For example, the codomain could be:

the real numbers,
an additive group,
a vector space,
or another explicitly declared structure.

This is a formalization gap.

It does not invalidate the concrete numerical instance.

---

## 8. What the derived difference establishes

Inside the numerical example:

Delta L(A,C)
=
L(C)-L(A)

is derived from the two reference readings.

Therefore no separate primitive value for Delta L(A,C) is required in that example.

SURVIVES.

However, DMO-001 does not prove that every possible binary or higher-order relation among MADELEINE states can be reconstructed from one scalar L.

The frozen demo itself correctly avoids that stronger claim.

---

## 9. Failure-criterion audit

DMO-001 declares explicit criteria of failure.

This is methodologically useful.

However, unlike later demos, it does not contain:

an executable,
an exhaustive finite sweep,
a parameter atlas,
or an adversarial battery exercising all failure clauses.

The demonstration is manually inspectable and internally reproducible as a conceptual construction.

Its reproducibility strength is therefore lower than DMO-002 through DMO-006.

This does not make the construction false.

It limits the evidential force of the demo.

---

## 10. Change of reference is not tested

Relabeling states is not the same operation as changing the reference origin.

DMO-001 tests:

A -> X,
B -> Y,
C -> Z

while retaining the same reading assignments.

It does not test a transformation:

sub_0 -> sub_1

together with the corresponding transformation of all readings.

Therefore DMO-001 does not establish reference-change covariance or origin independence.

A future formalization may study that question separately.

---

## 11. Relation to the wider D0 architecture

In Tomo I, sub_0 appears inside a larger structural expression involving:

R_sub,
reading modulators,
and perpetuity.

DMO-001 audits only the minimal common-reference role.

It does not establish from this example alone:

the necessity of R_sub,
the necessity of perpetuity,
the uniqueness of D0,
or the indispensable role of sub_0 in later dynamics.

Those are system-level questions.

## 12. Strongest justified statement

The strongest justified statement is:

Within the declared MADELEINE convention, sub_0 can serve consistently as the designated common internal reference for a family of relative readings. The numerical DMO-001 example allows some relations between readings to be derived from their values relative to that reference and is invariant under value-preserving relabeling of the marks.

This statement does not require sub_0 to be a physical object or an absolute point of physical space.

---

## 13. Statements not justified

DMO-001 does not establish that:

- sub_0 is physically real;
- sub_0 is an absolute point of physical space;
- sub_0 is mathematically unique;
- sub_0 is forced by the underlying mathematics;
- no alternative reference could be chosen;

- relabeling invariance implies reference-change invariance;
- sub_0 is indispensable to all later MADELEINE dynamics;
- a new geometry has been established.
