## 4. Historical information versus N

The LAB-D2 atlas audit establishes
an analytic relation under the
stationary pair-uniform assumptions.

Let p denote the conditional
five-symbol successor distribution.

Then:

I = ln(5) - H(p).

The trigram entropy satisfies:

H3 = ln(25) + H(p).

The normalized effective
trigram diversity is:

N = exp(H3)/125.

Consequently:

N = exp(H(p))/5

and:

N = exp(-I).

These are theoretical identities
under their declared assumptions.

Finite-sample estimates
need not satisfy them exactly.

Thus N cannot automatically
be counted as independent
emergent novelty alongside I.

VERDICT:

ANALYTIC DEPENDENCE DOCUMENTED.
