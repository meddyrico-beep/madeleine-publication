## 8. Failure and decision rules

A future claim of dynamic necessity
fails if the relevant observable
or transition factors through
a declared simpler representation.

A future uniqueness claim fails
if a valid inequivalent alternative
meets the same declared conditions.

A structure-preservation claim
fails if its required operation
does not commute with translation.

A claimed general soundness
guarantee fails if admitted evidence
supports only a narrower statement
than the promoted claim.

A universal external claim
cannot be accepted using only
internally generated synthetic data.

These are decision criteria,
not observations of new failures.
