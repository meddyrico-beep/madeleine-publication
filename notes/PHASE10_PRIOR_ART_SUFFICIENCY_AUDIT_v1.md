## 5. Six-channel comparison gap

DMO-003 supports a distinction
between source-channel states
that share a signed projection.

A novelty assessment must compare
the exact admissible representation
with established signed-coordinate
decompositions, quotient mappings
and related state encodings.

The relevant comparison needs:

- identical or comparable domains;
- equivalence and non-equivalence tests;
- actual downstream operators;
- demonstrable consequences.

A terminology difference alone
does not establish new mathematics.

VERDICT:
TARGETED PRIOR ART REVIEW REQUIRED.

## 6. Double-register comparison gap

- phase or cyclic counters;
- state-pair encodings.

The canonical register and
the finite demonstration must

VERDICT:
TARGETED PRIOR ART REVIEW REQUIRED.
